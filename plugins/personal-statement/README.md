# personal-statement

Write, upgrade and stress-test personal statements, statements of purpose, motivation letters, scholarship essays and short application answers, for undergraduate, graduate and job applicants. Keeps the applicant's own story and voice, checks every fact, debates content choices and preps interview or video answers. Use when the user shares a statement to improve, wants one written from their material, or needs interview prep.
