# personal-statement

Write, upgrade and stress-test personal statements, statements of purpose, motivation letters, scholarship essays, short-answer application questions and self-introductions, for school leavers, graduate and PhD applicants, scholarships and jobs. Keeps the applicant's own story and voice, checks every fact, debates content choices before and after drafting, and prepares interview or video answers. Use when the user shares a statement to improve, wants one written from their material, asks if something belongs in it, wants current facts added, or needs interview prep from it.
