# Personal Statement Frameworks

## PS Quick Reference

| Application Type | Typical Length | Core Focus | Key Differentiator |
|------------------|---------------|------------|-------------------|
| Graduate / Master's | 500–1000 words | Academic fit, career goals | Why this program specifically |
| PhD Program | 1000–2000 words | Research interest, methodology, advisor fit | Demonstrated research capability |
| Scholarship | 500–800 words | Merit, impact, financial need | Measurable achievements and future potential |
| Job Application | 300–500 words | Skills, experience, role fit | Concrete contributions and value proposition |
| Fellowship | 800–1500 words | Leadership, community impact, vision | Social impact and long-term commitment |

---

## Content Pillars Framework

Build your personal statement around 3-5 pillars that align with your strengths, experiences, and the target opportunity. Each pillar should be supported by concrete evidence.

### Example for a Graduate Applicant

| Pillar | % of Content | What to Include |
|--------|--------------|-----------------|
| Personal profile | 10% | Name, age, hometown, hobbies, personality traits, brief self-positioning |
| Academic foundation | 20% | Key coursework, GPA highlights, intellectual turning points |
| Research / project experience | 30% | Methodology, findings, publications, technical skills gained |
| Motivation & fit | 20% | Why this field, why this program, specific faculty/resources |
| Personal growth | 10% | Challenges overcome, cross-cultural experience, leadership |
| Future vision | 10% | Career goals, how the program bridges current skills to aspirations |

### Pillar Development Questions

| Pillar | Key Question to Ask |
|--------|--------------------|
| Personal profile | What traits, hobbies, or background elements define you beyond academics? |
| Academic foundation | Which courses or moments sparked a genuine shift in your thinking? Cite honors/rankings. |
| Research / project | What was YOUR specific role, methodology, and quantifiable outcome? |
| Motivation & fit | Why this program over others? Name faculty, labs, courses. Any direct exposure? |
| Personal growth | What significant obstacle did you overcome, and what did it teach you? |
| Future vision | What is your 5-10 year goal, and how does this program bridge the gap? |

## Format Templates by Scenario

Choose the appropriate format based on the application context:

| Scenario | Format | Key Feature | Typical Length |
|----------|--------|-------------|----------------|
| Graduate / PhD PS | Essay | Continuous prose, no headings or salutations | 500–1000 words |
| Motivation Letter (European) | Formal Letter | Sender/receiver address, salutation, signature | 1–2 pages |
| Statement of Purpose (SoP) | Research Essay | Research-focused sections, advisor/lab alignment | 1000–2000 words |
| Job Application | Short-Form Intro | Concise, competency-driven, role-specific | 300–500 words |
| Specific Essay Prompts | Q&A Response | Context → Action → Result → Connection per question | Varies by prompt |
