# humanizer

Make any piece of writing sound natural and like its author instead of machine-written - personal statements, reports, thesis chapters, CVs, cover letters, emails, posts - in English or Indonesian. Diagnoses AI-sounding words, rhythm and structure, asks whether to also upgrade the content, then rewrites at the lightest touch while keeping every fact, number and citation. Use when the user says humanize, humanizer, make it sound human or natural, sounds like AI, kayak AI, kurang natural, terlalu kaku, pastes an AI-detector report (GPTZero, Turnitin, ZeroGPT), or runs /humanizer.
